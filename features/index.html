Record & Analyze 01–02

Trade Journal

Every trade with pair, direction, size, P&L, R-multiple, MAE/MFE, session and hour. Add notes, tags, emotions, checklists, commission and swap, and screenshots.

  • Auto-graded A-F on R-multiple, execution, timing and management
  • Search, filter and sort by pair, session, direction and date range
  • Cumulative P&L column shows your equity curve inline
  • Trade replay with play/pause and running stats
  • Export to PDF or CSV

Edge Report

Ranks your top 5 places to make and lose money by session, hour, day and pair, with dollar amounts on each one. Export it as PDF or CSV.

  • Bias-alignment and volatility breakdowns
  • Tag analytics for your own setups
  • Post-close continuation analysis
  • What to Cut advisor: what's costing you, with numbers
  • Optional AI recommendations with your own Anthropic API key: 3-5 ranked suggestions with dollar estimates, from summary statistics only, and only when you click Run Analysis
Live Intelligence 03

Pulse — Live Session Intelligence

A live session clock with time remaining, an economic calendar with "danger zones" around big news, and a signal that tells you whether the session that's open matches your best history.

  • Session intelligence: FOCUS / OK / REDUCE / AVOID
  • Danger zones before high-impact news
  • Weekly economic calendar
  • Currency strength bars
  • Optional live prices from Twelve Data, Polygon.io, OANDA, or MetaTrader 5 directly (no key needed)
Risk & Timing 04–06
Risk analysis

Monte Carlo & Risk of Ruin

Run 1,000 randomized simulations of your trade history. See your probability of hitting a target and your risk of blowing the account.

  • 1,000 simulated equity curves with 5th-95th percentile bands
  • Probability of profit, target hit and drawdown
  • Risk of Ruin with the Kelly criterion
  • Exit efficiency: how much you leave on the table
  • Commission & swap cost tracking by symbol

Timing Analytics

When do you trade best? A P&L calendar, hourly and day-of-week breakdowns, and duration analysis show when your edge is sharpest.

  • Trade calendar with daily P&L
  • Hourly P&L chart: see which hours pay you
  • Day-of-week breakdown
  • Duration analysis: are you holding too long or cutting too short?
  • Trade time heatmap: hour vs. day grid
Timing edge

Sessions & Kill Zones

See P&L by session and hour, find your most profitable time windows, and understand your win/loss streaks.

  • Session heatmap: P&L by hour and day of week
  • Kill Zone Optimizer: your most profitable time windows
  • Session intelligence with per-session edge signals
  • Sequence analysis: win/loss streak patterns
  • Streak timeline: see momentum shifts
Deep Analysis 07–09

Pair & Instrument Analytics

What you trade and how: win/loss distributions, per-pair metrics, directional bias, lot sizing patterns and correlation.

  • Win/loss distribution with R-multiple histogram
  • Per-pair analytics: win rate, average P&L, trade count
  • Directional edge: long vs. short performance
  • Lot size analysis: are you sizing up after losses?
  • Pair correlation matrix
  • MAE/MFE and slippage analysis

Behavioral Analytics

Know your discipline score, your consistency score, and whether you're improving or slipping.

  • Discipline Score (A-F): overtrading, revenge, tilt, sizing
  • Consistency Score: win rate stability, recovery, timing
  • Trade grades: A-F distribution across your history
  • Emotional P&L: Calm, Confident, Anxious, Frustrated, Revenge, FOMO, Bored
  • Checklist compliance: dollar impact of following your rules

Risk & Growth Projections

Understand your drawdowns, project your account growth, and size positions from your actual numbers.

  • Drawdown recovery: time underwater, depth, behavior during drawdown
  • Growth projections with compounding scenarios
  • Position sizer based on your own risk metrics
  • Sharpe and Calmar ratios, benchmark comparison
  • Event Impact Profiler: how NFP, FOMC, CPI, ECB and BOE days affect your P&L
Connect 10

Import from the platforms you use

Drag and drop a CSV and Dojix detects the format, or import from OANDA through its API. Re-importing the same file never creates duplicates.

  • MetaTrader 5 (StrategyAnalytics CSV), with automatic import from your MT5 files folder
  • MetaTrader 4, cTrader, TradingView, NinjaTrader
  • OANDA, by CSV or API
  • Interactive Brokers, thinkorswim
  • Any other CSV through a column-mapping screen
Analytics

More than 60 analytics

Each one answers a question about your trading. An Essential view shows only the core set.

Core Analytics

Equity Curve & Returns

Equity curve with drawdown, monthly returns, cumulative P&L tracking.

Sharpe & Calmar

Risk-adjusted return ratios and a benchmark comparison. See whether your returns justify the risk you're taking.

Drawdown Recovery

Time underwater, the deepest drawdowns, and how you trade during drawdown vs. recovery.

Win/Loss Distribution

Histogram of trade outcomes with R-multiple buckets. See the shape of your edge and spot outlier winners and losers.

Commission & Fee Tracking

Total costs, cost per trade, cost by symbol. Know what your broker is really charging you.

Timing & Sessions

Session Heatmap

P&L by hour and day of week in a grid. See where your edge concentrates.

Kill Zone Optimizer

Groups adjacent profitable hours into windows to trade and windows to avoid, with a monthly P&L projection.

Hourly P&L Chart

P&L per trading hour (UTC). See which hours pay you and which cost you.

Day-of-Week Analysis

P&L broken down by Monday through Friday. Find your best and worst days.

Behavioral

Discipline Score (A-F)

A composite score across overtrading, revenge trading, tilt, and lot size increases after losses.

Consistency Score

Win rate stability over time, recovery from drawdowns, timing consistency. Are you getting better or worse?

Emotional P&L

Tag trades with your emotional state (Calm, Confident, Anxious, Frustrated, Revenge, FOMO, Bored) and see P&L per emotion.

Checklist Compliance

Define your trading rules and check them per trade. See the dollar impact of following vs. breaking them.

Risk & Projections

Monte Carlo Simulation

1,000 randomized equity curves with 5th/25th/median/75th/95th percentile bands and the probability of hitting a profit target.

Risk of Ruin (Kelly)

Kelly criterion and a risk gauge: your mathematical probability of blowing the account.

Growth Projections

Project account growth from your current metrics with adjustable compounding assumptions.

Edge & Events

AI Recommendations (optional)

With your own Anthropic API key, the Edge Report writes 3-5 ranked recommendations with dollar estimates. Only summary statistics are sent, never individual trades or notes, and only when you click Run Analysis.

Event Impact Profiler

Performance by event type (NFP, FOMC, CPI, ECB, BOE): win rate, average P&L and a TRADE / CAUTION / AVOID call per event.

What to Cut Advisor

Finds what's costing you money and how much, with a dollar amount on every item.

And More

Prop Firm Tracker

Set account size, profit target, daily loss limit and max drawdown, and track any prop-firm challenge with progress bars.

Playbook Catalog

Tag-based setup tracking with per-setup metrics: keep, review or drop each setup.

Plus many more

Pair correlation, exit efficiency, sequence analysis, directional edge, slippage, MAE/MFE, edge decay, time to MFE, net currency exposure, strategy checkpoints and more.
